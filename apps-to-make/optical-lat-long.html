Solar Fix

optical latitude / longitude estimate

Use a level photograph with a visible horizon. Set the camera’s true-north heading, mark the Sun, then mark the horizon at the Sun’s horizontal position.

This is a geometric draft, not a navigation instrument. Lens distortion, magnetic heading, refraction, and timestamp errors can move the result substantially.
