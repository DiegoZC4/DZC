VoiceSu live formant tracking

The Lab uses the upstream VoiceSu LPC, MFCC and trained Gaussian-mixture readout.
The browser loads revision cc54abd3887eca90a3c096e0bfc8ca446c0d4e1e.
https://github.com/brentenjackson/voicesu

VocalTrack smoothing adaptation

The legacy smoother in analysis.js and the previous LPC estimator in
live_tracker.js are retained for comparisons. The live worker uses the
SignalMeter from live_tracker.js for its signal-to-noise threshold.

The stability gate, log-frequency 1-Euro equations and default parameters in
analysis.js are adapted from Santiago Barreda's VocalTrack Smoother.py.
Source revision: abf77249eb8c05fe76a5d5fab3c9e1b442f8901a
https://github.com/santiagobarreda/VocalTrack

Legacy JavaScript adaptations: only F1/F2 are smoothed; real capture timestamps determine
the filter step; silence/uncertainty/mute/gaps reset it immediately; a stable
new vowel can reacquire after three consistent estimates. No pitch normalization.
