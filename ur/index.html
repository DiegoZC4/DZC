    Race. Capture. Escape.

    Get every piece off the far end of your route before your opponent. Each marked die adds one step. Move one piece the entire roll; you may jump over other pieces.

    • Click a highlighted piece to move, or its destination. Click your plain pile to bring a new piece onto the board. Each overlapping circle is one piece; the pile with inset rings holds your finished pieces. Hover either pile for its exact count.
    • Landing on an opponent sends their piece back to the start. Your own pieces block a landing, except when stacking is allowed.
    • Leaving the board requires an exact roll. A zero or no legal move passes the turn automatically.
    • The five double-outlined squares are the rosettes: their protection and extra-roll rules depend on the variant below.
    • Use Route to see the current player’s path. On Tournament Engine, select a piece, then a forward or backward destination.
    Four variants
    RulesPieces / diceRosettesTwist
    Finkel7 / 4Safe; roll again14-square route

    Tournament5 / 4Safe; no extra roll16 squares; stacks; backwards

    Tournament: friendly pieces merge only on rosettes. A stack moves intact, scores together, and breaks into separate pieces when captured. Backwards moves are optional; passing is allowed only without a legal forward move. Pieces cannot retreat off the starting edge.

    These are modern playable reconstructions and variants, not a claim that one ancient ruleset is known with certainty. An automatic, tied-roll-repeated roll-off chooses the starter; the first move uses a fresh roll.
