Resize Axes

Click any tipew-resize on top

The four two-sided resize cursors intersect in the center. Each pair of opposite tips belongs to one axis: ew, ns, nesw, or nwse.

col-resizeColumn divider resize.
row-resizeRow divider resize.

Single Direction Resize

nw-resizeNorthwest corner.
n-resizeNorth edge.
ne-resizeNortheast corner.
w-resizeWest edge.
The compass layout matches the direction in the cursor name.
e-resizeEast edge.
sw-resizeSouthwest corner.
s-resizeSouth edge.
se-resizeSoutheast corner.

These are useful when a resize handle is attached to a specific edge or corner. Browsers may draw some of them as two-headed arrows, depending on the operating system.
