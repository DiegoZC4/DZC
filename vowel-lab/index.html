The diamond plays the two resonances under your cursor at your last sung pitch. Before your first note it uses A3. With the chart focused, use arrow keys to move and hold Space to listen.

The microphone dot shows F1 and F2 in Hz, estimated by linear prediction from the shape of your spectrum. At high pitches, widely spaced harmonics make the resonances harder to locate. A hollow dot marks these estimates.
