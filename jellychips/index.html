Cutting, accuracy & sources

Laser settings

Import the SVG at 1:1 in millimetres. The red hairline is the only cut geometry. Kerf is the full measured cut width; the exported centerline is offset outward by half that value. Keep kerf at zero here if your laser software will compensate instead.

Kerf starts at zero because your machine has not been calibrated. Test a coupon in the actual sheet. Stock thickness, taper, corner rounding, sealing, silicone and cooling all affect the final result. This is not a dimensional-accuracy guarantee.

⅛ inch is 3.175 mm, but nominal sheets can differ: enter the measured thickness. Thinner stock is generally easier to cut; your machine and plywood must still be approved for laser use.

Casting & cutting

Cut the indicated number of identical outlines, align and stack them into a positive master. The stack provides cavity depth, not the final jelly thickness. Pour the weighed batch into a level mold; do not fill to the rim unless that is your calibrated volume.

After demolding onto a normal food cutting board, score every grid direction before separating pieces. Squares use two directions; triangles use three. Use food-contact silicone following its maker’s instructions; this is not a food-contact specification for plywood or its sealer. Wood can inhibit silicone curing: test your finished, sealed master before making the full mold.

The idealized height uses mass ÷ density ÷ cell area. Equal mass also requires uniform thickness/density and negligible cutting or moisture losses. Triangle side length means the length of each of its three edges.

The default 1.080 g/mL is a rough planning proxy: the cited 20% sugar-in-water reference is 1.08093 g/mL at 20 °C. It is not a measured agar-gel density. Enter your own measured value for your recipe if precise height matters.

Search & privacy

Search runs in a single Web Worker on this computer. No designs are uploaded. It checks exact cell count, one connected body, no holes, no point contacts, and a corner at every boundary grid point. Results are ranked by their actual smallest enclosing circle; compactness does not guarantee symmetry or a minimum neck width. Stop preserves completed candidates.

Settings and layouts are saved in this browser when storage is available. Side length, mass and kerf do not require a new layout search.
