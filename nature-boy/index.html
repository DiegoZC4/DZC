About this rehearsal copy

The player follows the tempo changes in Blake’s MuseScore typesetting and scales them proportionally with the tempo control. Playback is synthesized from the notation, which is what makes true per-part mute and solo possible.

This page is intended for choir rehearsal. The underlying arrangement remains credited to Anders Edenroth.
