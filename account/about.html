DiegoZC

Reading tools for following along with audio and keeping notes on selected passages.

The readers

  • Sequences read-along: weekly reading-group audio and text, with private annotations associated with your account.
  • The Metropolitan Man: chapter-based audio and text, with public passage comments for the reading group.

Google sign-in and saved annotations are being prepared. The existing readers remain available without an account.

One sign-in, no profile

The optional shared Google sign-in recognizes the same account across these readers and devices. It requests only openid. The website keeps a keyed, opaque account identifier so it can find your annotations and ensure that other users cannot edit them.

No Google email address, name, profile picture, birthday, contacts, Gmail or Drive access is requested. Annotations are stored on the website's hosting, not in Google. Public comments use a generated Reader label rather than your Google identity.

See the Privacy Policy for the exact data, visibility, storage and deletion rules, and the Terms of Service for use of the annotation feature.
